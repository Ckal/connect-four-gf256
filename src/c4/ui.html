These percentages are not a winning chance. Each number is the model's preference weight for one column (softmax(hard_forward)), i.e. how the probability mass is split across the legal columns — not an estimate of who wins the game. A 100% simply means the model put all its weight on one column (this normally happens when only one column is legal); it does not mean a certain win. 100% can also appear while the position is lost.
